## Row-by-Row Notes

### Row 2 — El Salvador: Korea Innovation Nexus for R&D Collaboration

**Organization:** KOICA  
**Project:** Korea Innovation Nexus for R&D Collaboration  
**Investment recorded:** $9,000,000  
**Financial type:** Development Cooperation

The Salvadoran Foreign Ministry reports that ESCO and KOICA established the Korea Innovation Nexus for R&D Collaboration at the University of El Salvador. The project includes a technology innovation center with laboratories for automation, prototyping, digital fabrication, and quality control, supported by $9 million in non-reimbursable Korean financial cooperation.

**Primary source:**  
[Ministerio de Relaciones Exteriores de El Salvador](https://rree.gob.sv/el-salvador-y-corea-trabajan-de-la-mano-por-la-innovacion-y-el-desarrollo-tecnologico/)

### Row 3 — El Salvador: Center for Industrial Innovation Training at ITCA-FEPADE

**Organization:** KOICA  
**Project:** Center for Industrial Innovation Training at ITCA-FEPADE  
**Investment recorded:** $5,800,000  
**Financial type:** Development Cooperation

The dataset records the Korean cooperation project associated with ITCA-FEPADE and industrial innovation training. The project includes Industry 4.0-related training and technical capabilities involving areas such as industrial robotics, mechatronics, electronics, programming, cybersecurity, and smart manufacturing.

**Primary source:**  
[Ministerio de Relaciones Exteriores de El Salvador — El Salvador avanza a una nueva era industrial con la cooperación de Corea del Sur](https://rree.gob.sv/el-salvador-avanza-hacia-una-nueva-era-industrial-con-la-cooperacion-de-corea-del-sur/)

**Dataset note:** The project was initiated in 2021, while the Salvadoran government documentation used for the dataset was published later. The dataset's `Year` field therefore refers to the project period rather than the publication date.

### Row 5 — Mexico: Kia EV3 Production & Electromobility Expansion

**Organization:** Kia Mexico  
**Project:** Kia EV3 Production & Electromobility Expansion  
**Investment recorded:** $649,000,000  
**Financial type:** Corporate FDI

The Mexican federal government announced a $649 million Kia investment for 2026–2028 to establish EV3 production in Pesquería, Nuevo León. The project also includes a national charging network, a 2 MW solar park, and a wastewater-treatment facility.

[Presidencia de la República](https://www.gob.mx/presidencia/prensa/plan-mexico-presidenta-destaca-inversion-de-kia-de-649-mdd-para-produccion-de-vehiculo-electrico-muestra-la-confianza-en-el-pais)

**Organization:** LG Magna e-Powertrain  
**Project:** Ramos Arizpe EV Powertrain Manufacturing Plant  
**Investment recorded:** —

### Row 10 — United States: New Carlisle EV Battery Manufacturing Plant

**Organization:** Samsung SDI  
**Project:** New Carlisle EV Battery Manufacturing Plant  
**Investment recorded:** $3,500,000,000  
**Financial type:** Corporate FDI / Joint Venture

Samsung SDI and General Motors established a joint venture for an EV battery manufacturing facility in New Carlisle, Indiana. The original project announcement described approximately $3.5 billion in investment, 27 GWh of initial annual capacity, and more than 1,600 jobs.

## Source Method

Government sources were prioritized for government-supported projects and public investment announcements. Official company sources were used when the company itself was the primary source of project-specific information. When multiple sources reported different investment figures, the dataset favors the most directly attributable and recent project-specific figure. When a reliable amount could not be established, the investment field was left blank rather than estimated.
